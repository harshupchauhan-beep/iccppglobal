Mission / Language Course

Mission

Language courses for international work.

ICCPP members may take free German lessons in individual or group sessions (WhatsApp and Skype). The course aims at a level comparable to Goethe-Institut C1.

Format

How the course is taught

Lessons prepare members to study or work internationally. Progress is recorded as a running word count of written German.
