Hans-Werner Gessmann

The internships page on iccpp.org points to the English Wikipedia article on Hans-Werner Gessmann. That encyclopaedia entry stays on Wikipedia; this page keeps the portrait and the outbound link so nothing from the internships supervisor block is dropped.

Prof. Dr. Hans-Werner Gessmann (1950–2023) was a German clinical psychologist, founder of the Psychotherapeutic Institute Bergerhausen, and long-standing Director of ICCPP. The Centre lists him as agency supervisor for the student internship programme.
