What this website stores when you write to the Centre or submit a paper.

Controller

Who is responsible

The original notice named Prof. Dr. Hans-Werner Gessmann, D-86551 Aichach, Germany, as controller. Current contact for the Centre is telephone +91 81306 73015 and iccppglobal@gmail.com.

What this site stores

  • Contact, internship, newsletter, and feedback forms store the name, email, and message you type, plus the time of submission.
  • A paper submission stores the author name, email, title, abstract, payment reference, and the PDF if you upload one.
  • The network address of a submission is kept for a short time so the form can refuse a burst of automated messages.
  • Card and UPI details are handled by Razorpay. This website stores the payment reference, not the card number.
  • Centre staff can see these records in the admin inbox. Write to iccppglobal@gmail.com to ask for access, correction, or deletion.

What the original policy covers

  • Log data when the site is visited: IP address, time, URL, referrer, browser — for connection, security, and stability (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR).
  • No use of logs to identify you beyond those purposes.
  • Session and temporary cookies for usability; statistics cookies as described in the live notice. You may block cookies in the browser.
  • Rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to a supervisory authority (Arts. 15–20, 7 (3), 77 GDPR).
  • Objection under Art. 21 GDPR; the original notice invited mail to haertl@kanzlei-haertl.de.
  • TLS encryption and organisational security measures, reviewed as technology changes.

The list above describes this website. The 2018 notice remains the earlier statement of the Centre’s data-protection duties.
