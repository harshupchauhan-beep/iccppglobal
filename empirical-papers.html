How to structure empirical term papers

Keep data and argument distinct. ICCPP expects a conventional empirical skeleton.

  • Introduction: problem, relevance, and question.
  • Literature: theories and prior findings that justify the method.
  • Method: sample, instruments, procedure, analysis plan.
  • Results: findings without interpretation.
  • Discussion: meaning, limits, and implications for practice.
  • References in a consistent academic style.
