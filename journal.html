Journal of Clinical Psychology and Psychotherapy

ICCPP Journal is the peer-reviewed and indexed journal. It is the official publication of the International Center for Clinical Psychology and Psychotherapy (ICCPP). All articles are evaluated according to several dimensions: originality of the work, scientific reasoning, writing style, references, citations. Authors are advised to check for plagiarism before texts are sent to the editors.
