Criteria for the evaluation of theses

ICCPP criteria emphasise clarity of question, ethical procedure, appropriate method, and honest reporting of limits.

  • The question is specific and answerable with the chosen method.
  • Ethics and data protection are described and followed.
  • Instruments and analysis match the question.
  • Results are reported without over-claiming.
  • Language is precise; sources are complete.
