Journal guide

How the ICCPP Journal handles submission, review, price, and access.

About this journal

The ICCPP Journal of Clinical Psychology and Psychotherapy is the Centre’s peer-reviewed publication, issued by Verlag des PIB in Aichach. Online ISSN 2747-6464. Print ISSN 2941-5500.

Editors consider originality, scientific reasoning, writing, and citations. Authors should check for plagiarism before sending a manuscript.

Goals and scope

The journal covers clinical psychology, psychotherapy, psychodiagnostics, and related human sciences.

How a paper is reviewed

Payment starts the submission. It does not guarantee publication. Centre staff read the manuscript, and an accepted paper appears on the journal page only after an editor publishes it.

Submit a paper

Authors, ethics, and conduct

Send only work you have the right to submit. Do not include sensitive clinical information that identifies a patient. Questions can go to verlagpib@gmail.com.

The editorial and reviewer names are listed on the members page.

Article processing charge and orders

The article processing charge is 5000 INR, shown on the submit form before you pay. Razorpay processes the card or UPI payment. ICCPP does not store your card number.

Reading the journal

Accepted papers and issue covers on this site are free to read. For a print copy or a library order, write to iccppglobal@gmail.com.
