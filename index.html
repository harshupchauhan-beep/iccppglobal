03 — News

Leadership of the Centre

3 October 2023 · Director’s announcement

Vishal Lohchab appointed Director

Professor Hans-Werner Gessmann, founder and long-standing Director of ICCPP, died on 3 October 2023. His work in humanistic psychodrama and clinical training shaped the Centre for decades.

Vishal Lohchab, previously scientific associate to Professor Gessmann, now directs ICCPP. He oversees operations, publications, and international programmes, with the support of the Gessmann family and the Centre’s administration.
