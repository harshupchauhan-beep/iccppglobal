Exposé

State the research question, theoretical frame, method, and expected contribution before a thesis or paper is written in full.

  • Title and working question, including population and setting.
  • Brief literature map: what is known and where the gap sits.
  • Proposed method (qualitative, quantitative, or mixed) and instruments.
  • Ethics: consent, confidentiality, and risk.
  • Timetable and what the study is expected to add.
